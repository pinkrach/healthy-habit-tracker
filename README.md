# Healthy Habit Tracker

A single-page tracker for a 4-week stacking habit plan, from Saturday, October 10 through Friday, November 6, 2026.

Each week adds one habit and keeps every habit from earlier weeks. The weekly goal is 5 successful days out of 7 for each unlocked habit. Checkmarks are saved in this browser with `localStorage`.

## Habits

| Week | Dates | New habit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sat Oct 10 – Fri Oct 16 | Add 1 serving of produce daily |
| 2 | Sat Oct 17 – Fri Oct 23 | Reach 8 glasses (64 oz) of water daily |
| 3 | Sat Oct 24 – Fri Oct 30 | Swap 1 refined grain for whole grain daily |
| 4 | Sat Oct 31 – Fri Nov 6 | Limit sweets to 1 intentional portion daily |

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then visit `http://localhost:4173`.
